## Findings (2 blocker · 6 major · 3 minor)

1. **BLOCKER: real brand on the octagon.** src/03d_lexington.js:2-17 and 247-329, and src/09l_lexington.js:13-14 and 32-33. They ship the real **HOOTERS** wordmark in its own orange channel-letter styling, a **® mark** (03d:262) and the **owl mascot** (03d:264-270, 316). HOMAGE.md says "No logos, wordmarks… real brand characters." **Fix:** rename it to an original wing joint (for example "HOOT HOLLOW WINGS"). Use original lettering, drop the ®, and draw an original bird or none. Rename `hooters`/`HOOTERS_ANCHOR` in ids and blurbs.
2. **BLOCKER: Hard Rock Cafe logo (older content in this snapshot).** src/03b_landmarks_hd.js:246-390. `hrLogo` draws the real Hard Rock Cafe roundel and logo on the guitar and the fascia. **Fix:** replace it with an original roundel and name. The comment at 03b:86 cites a "real-signage rule, 2026-10-11" that contradicts HOMAGE.md, and **Brad needs to rule on which one wins.**
3. **MAJOR: other real brand names.** src/06e_gr1_data.js:3 (landmarks: "M&T BANK STADIUM", "DOMINO SUGARS", "UNDER ARMOUR") and src/03_city.js (16 hits). These are building names, but they are trademarks. **Fix:** use generic names ("THE STADIUM", "SUGAR REFINERY", "TIDE POINT"), or get Brad's sign-off.
4. **MAJOR: Pete's eat-off lint failures.** src/09d_pete.js:37 and 277 use `Math.random` (C2). 09d:158 adds a `PointLight` (C4). 09d:161-188 create their own Mesh*Material (C3), and 09d:188 sets frustumCulled false (C7). **Fix:** use seeded `rnd`, a shared toonMat/glow mat and an emissive card instead of the light. This one was carried over from R-034 and is still open.
5. **MAJOR: Lexington night glows break the material rule.** src/03d_lexington.js:34 creates its own `MeshBasicMaterial` per glow quad (C3). **Fix:** share one additive glow material from the atlas, and drive the glow with per-instance opacity or vertex alpha.
6. **MAJOR: spot popups ignore other modes.** src/09l_lexington.js:16-23 runs `spotTick` on every MODE tick without checking `MODE.duel`, Fort, derby, racers or Vic. When Harold or the camera is near the octagon (Rash Field is next door) during those modes, the `#lmk` name can pop up over their HUDs. **Fix:** return early when `MODE.cur`/`DUELS.cur`/the full map is active.
7. **MAJOR: Pete anchor wiring is fragile.** src/09d_pete.js:431 and 451-454. 03d:47 defines LEX_ANCHOR, but Pete's ring falls back to `TEMP_ANCHOR` near Oriole Park if the anchor isn't there yet, and it only re-binds while tick n < 600. Bundle order puts 03d before 09d, so the real anchor should win. Start and end at the real anchor are **UNVERIFIED** at runtime. **Fix:** read LEX_ANCHOR straight away and delete the temp path. Then confirm with `?pete=1&peteauto=win` and `lose`.
8. **MAJOR: the Westminster popup may not fit on a phone.** site/index.html:194. `#lmk` is 15px, letter-spacing 1px, `nowrap`, plus "- " and " -". With them, "WESTMINSTER BURYING GROUND" is about 30 characters, roughly 330–360px against a 390px viewport (estimated, **UNVERIFIED**). **Fix:** shorten it to "WESTMINSTER GRAVES" or allow wrap below 420px.
9. **MINOR: draw-call risk.** src/03d_lexington.js has 61 mesh, box or cyl calls across 4 places, plus a separate canvas per Hooters sign face (03d:251, 309). Draw calls aren't measured. **UNVERIFIED:** needs a viewer readout per `?goto=` place. **Fix:** confirm that the H.* parts merge into the 64 m chunks (F6).
