## Description
The car drives itself. You get two buttons: LEFT holds the cold-gas fan (sonic loops),
RIGHT fires the thrusters (the kickflip over Teslas crossing the 4-way).
Starbase, one take, zero crashes. Built in three.js — play it free in the arcade.

Play: https://bparlette.github.io/Hawk/brad-demos/#roadster-flip
