MY GAME: [Describe it in 1 to 3 sentences. Name the game it's like, if any.]

Build this as one self-contained index.html I can open in a browser. Make it feel like a real game someone would play for 10 minutes, not a demo.

SPEND YOUR EFFORT HERE, IN THIS ORDER
1. The fantasy. Before coding, write 3 lines: what the player does every second, what makes it exciting, and what the big moment is. Build toward that.
2. Content. Real variety: several enemy types, weapons or moves, upgrades or levels, a boss or finale. More game beats more polish.
3. Look. Pick one strong art style and commit (toon 3D with outlines, painted 2D, pixel art, or neon). Give the player and main enemies detailed, recognizable models made from many shapes, not single boxes or cones. The player should be the brightest thing on screen.
4. Feel. Hits get screenshake, a flash, particles and a sound. Movement has weight.

ONLY THESE RULES ARE HARD
- Must be possible to win and to lose, with a results screen and a Retry that fully resets.
- Works on a phone (touch controls appear automatically, portrait works) and on a keyboard.
- No fake features: every button works, nothing claims to be something it isn't.
- Sounds made in code with Web Audio; no downloaded assets. three.js from one CDN link is fine.

Don't explain the rules back to me or write a checklist. Just output the full file, then a short list of controls.

---
FOLLOW-UP PROMPTS (send one at a time after it works)
- "Play it in your head as a first-time player. What's boring or confusing in the first 30 seconds? Fix it."
- "Make the player and enemy models twice as detailed and give the world more landmarks."
- "Add more content: 3 new enemy types and 3 new upgrades. Keep everything else working."
- "Add juice: hitstop, combo text, and a dramatic boss intro and death."
- "It lags on my phone. Find the slowest part and fix it without removing content."
