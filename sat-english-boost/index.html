Grammar rules first

A few focused sessions

  • Comma splices & sentence boundaries
  • Apostrophes
  • Subject–verb agreement
  • Pronoun clarity

CARD 4 · VOCAB

Words in Context

Use the passage, not word lists

Vocab questions are answered from the passage’s own clues — contrast, restatement, tone — far more reliably than from memorized definitions.

  • Read the sentence around the blank.
  • Look for a contrast word (but, however, unlike) or a restatement.
  • Pick the word that matches the clue, not the one you know best.

Read 20 minutes a day

Daily habit — builds stamina & vocab
