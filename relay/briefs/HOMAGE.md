# Baltimore homage rule (Brad, 2026-10-11) — applies to O5, O6, O7 and every new character

Brad wants the bosses to read instantly as Baltimore icons. Push the homage as close as possible WITHOUT copying a trademark.

Allowed: team colour schemes, silhouette type (a cartoon bird, a raven, a crab), regional props and attitude, original nicknames, original lettering.
Not allowed: official mascot designs or faces (the Oriole Bird, Poe/Rise/Conquer), team logos, cap "O"/bird logos, wordmarks, jersey numbers tied to real players, real brand characters (Utz girl, Natty Boh man, Old Bay tin, crab-house logos), or any copied image.

Direction per boss where it applies:
- Bird Brain Bobby: big-headed, cheeky orange-and-black cartoon bird in a ballcap with NO logo, baseball-mascot energy, bat or glove props.
- The Raven King: purple-and-black menacing raven, gold accents, football-mascot swagger, cape and crown, no team marks.
- Crab characters (Chef Crabcake etc.): classic Maryland blue crab, steamed-red when angry, a mallet and an original seasoning tin with original lettering.
- Others: lean into Baltimore types (hon beehive, arabber cart, rowhouse stoop, formstone), always original.

When unsure, choose the more original option and say so in your reply.
