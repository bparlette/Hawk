# Lanternport: script

## Premise
Lanternport taxes everything: bread, lamps, even the wind in a skiff's sail. Twin couriers Juniper and Fen
used to deliver the tax notices. Now they deliver everything else.

- **Juniper** (rooftop courier): quick, cheerful, cannot pass a ledge without climbing it.
- **Fen** (skiff pilot): dry, careful, drives like the opposite of careful.
- **The Lantern Watch**: tireless, polite, bad at stairs.
- **Quickstitch** (the tailor): sells alibis by the yard.

## Job 1: Hot Bread Run
*Beacon at the bakery. Teaches: getting in a ride, driving, timers.*

> **Card:** The baker owes us a favour. Their cart is the favour.

1. Borrow the baker's hover-cart. *(Prompt: F borrow the hover-cart.)*
2. Get the bread to the hideout while it is hot. *75 seconds.*

Barks. Juniper, getting in: "It still smells like breakfast." On a crash: "The crust is structural!"
Pass: "Warm. We're professionals."

## Job 2: Lamplighters
*Beacon by the terrace steps. Teaches: jumping, roofs, Heat, hiding.*

> **Card:** Five moon-lamps on the terrace roofs. Nobody will miss them until dark.

1. Collect the moon-lamps (0/5). *100 seconds. One on the ground, four up the terraces.*
2. The Watch saw the glow. Lose them. *Heat is set to two lanterns. Roofs and alleys break line of sight.*

Barks. Juniper, last lamp: "That's the set." Heat up: "And that's the Watch." Out of sight: "Up here they
never look. Nobody ever looks up." Pass: "Five lamps, zero witnesses. Well. Zero who can climb."

## Job 3: Tithe Day
*Beacon at the counting house. Teaches: ramming, pickups, the hand-off between heroes.*

> **Card:** The tithe wagon is slow, heavy, and full of other people's money.

1. Ram the tithe wagon until it drops the strongbox (0/3).
2. Grab the strongbox. *Heat jumps to three lanterns; Watch wagons join.*
3. Hand the strongbox to your twin at the docks.
4. *Control switches.* You are the other twin now. Lose the Watch.

Barks. Third ram: "There's the rattle." Hand-off, Juniper: "Yours!" Fen: "It's heavy." Juniper: "It's
everyone's." Pass, Fen: "We give it back in small, untraceable loaves."

## Short-video shot list (each 8 to 20 seconds, vertical)
Record with `?clean=1` so only the game HUD shows. Every shot is something the current build does.

1. **"Nobody looks up."** Three lanterns of Heat, wagon closing. Hero skids the cart into an alley, hops
   out, crate, ledge, roof. Guards mill about below while the green bar fills. Caption on "You lost them".
2. **The swap.** Juniper sprints into frame at the docks with the strongbox, camera rockets up over the
   town and drops onto Fen, who floors the skiff out of shot.
3. **Bread physics.** Start on the "Hot Bread Run" card, cut to a handbrake drift through a junction with
   bunting overhead, finish on "Job done +150".
4. **Triple-jump commute.** No vehicles: run, double, triple jump, wall kick up an alley, across three
   roofs at golden hour, dive off onto a moving cart's lane and press F.
5. **Polite pursuit.** A guard chases the hero in circles round a tree on the lawn. No caption needed.
6. **Postcard.** Slow orbit from a tall roof: lit windows, haze, mountains. Title card. Use as the hook
   frame and thumbnail.

Hook text options: "I made the cosy city where crime is bread." / "What if the getaway driver could
triple-jump?" / "Two heroes, one stolen bakery cart."
