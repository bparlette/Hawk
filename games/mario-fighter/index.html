Mario × Street Fighter

Ryu dropped into a full Super Mario World–style level — and he brought his whole move set with him. Stomp enemies Mario-style, or fight them: punch, kick, Hadouken, Shoryuken, even an air Tatsumaki. Punch bricks apart, kick Koopa shells down the course, and reach the flagpole.

Ryu's move set

Every move is animated from Ryu's real arcade frames and every one of them actually works in the level — on Goombas, Koopas, shells, and bricks.

How the level plays

Two ways to win a fightStomp enemies from above, or stand and fight them with punches, kicks, and specials. Both score.
Shells are weaponsStomp or hit a Koopa to shell it, then kick the shell — it slides and takes out everything in its path.
Blocks pay outBump ? blocks from below for coins. Bricks bump from below but break when you punch or kick them.
Three hearts, one flagPits cost a heart and drop you at the last safe ground. Clear the staircase and touch the flagpole for COURSE CLEAR.
