Detailed Stage Breakdown
Act I: The Street & The Dojo (Prologue)
 * Stage 01: Ryu's Dojo
   * Setting & Atmosphere: A misty mountainside dojo in Japan at dawn, opening the game's combat training arc.
   * Hazards & Flow: Pure fundamentals check with zero cheap stage hazards. Players walk right through cedar corridors, smashing training dummies and scroll racks to uncover tea and onigiri health pickups.
   * Enemies: Infiltrating Shadaloo scouts and rogue karateka testing the dojo's defenses.
   * Boss Encounter: Mid-stage sparring against Ken Masters (Noah Centineo) to learn parry timings and punish windows. The stage concludes with a shadowy Evil Ryu projection that unleashes hyper-fast Hadoukens and teleport dashes before vanishing at 10% health.
 * Stage 02: Metro City Streets
   * Setting & Atmosphere: Gritty, neon-drenched downtown streets and an active subway line overrun by the Mad Gear gang.
   * Hazards & Flow: Warning headlights signal incoming motorcycle drive-bys. Breakable trash cans and phone booths drop lead pipes and whole roast chickens. The stage transitions inside a speeding subway car where forward movement pushes players back toward trailing mob waves.
   * Enemies: Classic Final Fight archetypes—knife-wielding punks, agile kickers, and heavy Andore-style grapplers.
   * Boss Encounter: Balrog (50 Cent) in an underground boxing ring. Balrog charges with super-armor straights; players must sidestep his rushes and lure him into ring turnbuckles to stagger him.
 * Stage 03: E. Honda's Bathhouse
   * Setting & Atmosphere: Steamy, echoing traditional Japanese bathhouse leading into the furnace maintenance rooms.
   * Hazards & Flow: Periodic steam geysers cloud player vision and hide ambushing enemies. Wet floor tiles double knockback distance, allowing players to bounce foes off tiled walls. Smashed cedar buckets yield sushi and bento boxes.
   * Enemies: Sumo acolytes with linear charging headbutts and Foot-style infiltration ninja.
   * Boss Encounter: E. Honda (Hirooki Goto). His Hundred-Hand Slap shreds players at close range, while attempting back grabs triggers his punishing Ochio Throw. Players must bait his flying sumo splash and punish on recovery.
Act II: The Global Circuit (World Tour)
 * Stage 04: Guile's Airbase
   * Setting & Atmosphere: A sprawling USAF tarmac at sunset, with fighter jets warming up in open hangars.
   * Hazards & Flow: Jet blast exhaust zones push players backwards on a repeating siren cycle. Parked refueling trucks and fuel drums can be detonated by player strikes, incinerating clustered mob waves.
   * Enemies: Corrupted military police, Shadaloo saboteurs in flight gear, and attack dogs.
   * Boss Encounter: Guile (Cody Rhodes). Guile controls the horizontal lane with Sonic Booms and anti-airs jump-ins with invincible Flash Kicks. Players must advance along the vertical axis, slip under projectile paths, and punish whiffed kicks.
 * Stage 05: Blanka's Jungle
   * Setting & Atmosphere: The dense canopy of the Amazon basin, tracking along riverbanks under an escalating tropical thunderstorm.
   * Hazards & Flow: Auto-scrolling river path featuring rolling log obstacles and piranha-infested pools acting as instant-damage pits. When the storm breaks, rain slicks the mud and telegraphed lightning strikes fry marked zones on the floor.
   * Enemies: Feral mutant packs leaping from tree branches, accompanied by Shadaloo bio-trappers equipped with stun batons.
   * Boss Encounter: Blanka (Jason Momoa). Blanka attacks with rapid rolling dashes and ground-slam electricity that arcs across wet puddle surfaces. Under 25% health, he enters an enraged berserk state with increased speed and extended discharge radiuses.
 * Stage 06: Zangief's Steel Mill
   * Setting & Atmosphere: A roaring industrial steel plant in Novosibirsk filled with grinding machinery and glowing iron.
   * Hazards & Flow: Heavy overhead crane hooks swing across the screen on a pendulum timer, functioning as interactive hazards players can kick enemies into. Hydraulic press pistons slam down into glowing anvil zones, and open molten troughs inflict burn damage.
   * Enemies: Massive Siberian foundry wrestlers with command grabs and Shadaloo technicians throwing heavy spanners.
   * Boss Encounter: Zangief (Olivier Richters). Immune to flinching from standard light jabs; his Spinning Double Lariat reflects projectiles. His Spinning Piledriver (SPD) wipes out massive health, forcing players to hit-and-run while baiting missed grabs.
 * Stage 07: Vega's Cage
   * Setting & Atmosphere: A sun-drenched Spanish villa courtyard transitioning into an enclosed, vaulted steel cage arena.
   * Hazards & Flow: Players fight through matador guards across wide stone steps before entering the central locked cage. The perimeter chain-link fence allows players to climb and drop-kick down. Falling chandeliers progressively drop into corners, shrinking the safe floor area.
   * Enemies: Rapier-wielding matadors, acrobatic aerialists, and elite Shadaloo ceremonial guards.
   * Boss Encounter: Vega (Orville Peck). Vega scales the iron fencing to dive across the arena with his claw. At 50% health, his mask and claw shatter, initiating an enraged second phase with faster ground sprints and evasive backflips.
 * Stage 08: Dhalsim's Temple
   * Setting & Atmosphere: A dawn-lit stone sanctuary in Kerala surrounded by carved elephants and meditating ascetics.
   * Hazards & Flow: Meditating monks in the background emit golden auras; standing near them grants temporary speed or attack damage buffs. Balconies line the background where fire-breathers rain down lingering carpet-flame hazards.
   * Enemies: Yoga initiates using extended limb strikes from long range, accompanied by temple flame-dancers.
   * Boss Encounter: Dhalsim (Vidyut Jammwal). Dhalsim relies on teleport mix-ups, screen-spanning stretched punches, and lingering Yoga Flame traps that cordon off sections of the floor. Players must anticipate his reappearance point to trap him in combo loops.
Act III: The Shadow Conspiracy
 * Stage 09: Hong Kong Rooftops
   * Setting & Atmosphere: Rain-soaked neon rooftops and billboard gantries high above Kowloon, leading to a private helipad.
   * Hazards & Flow: Rooftop gap jumps test player platforming; mistimed leaps cause health loss. Smashed neon signs crash down with electrical shockwaves. Chun-Li (Callina Liang) fights alongside the team as an unhealable AI ally. The helipad finale features intense helicopter downdrafts dragging fighters toward the edge.
   * Enemies: Triad street operatives, roller-skating blade thugs, and Shadaloo sniper drones.
   * Boss Encounter: Vega's Lieutenant & The Doll Unit. A dual-boss tag match on the helipad requiring players to split focus between acrobatic claw rushes and coordinated dive kicks.
 * Stage 10: World Warrior Tournament Arena
   * Setting & Atmosphere: The centerpiece stadium of the 1993 underground tournament, surrounded by cheering crowds and camera booms.
   * Hazards & Flow: Players fight through preliminary brackets across the main arena floor before stepping into the central elevated ring. Elastic ring ropes grant slingshot rebounds for flying cross-body attacks. Players can smash corrupt official control booths for score multipliers and health drops.
   * Enemies: Disqualified tournament contestants, riot-shield Shadaloo enforcers, and corrupt judges.
   * Boss Encounter: M. Bison (Tournament). Fought in the center ring under tournament lights. Bison uses military slide kicks, teleport feints, and his signature Psycho Crusher charge across the ring.
Act IV: Finale & Secret Boss
 * Stage 11: Shadaloo Base
   * Setting & Atmosphere: Bison's subterranean island fortress, traversing the main surveillance center, armory hangar, and glowing Psycho Drive core.
   * Hazards & Flow: Corridors feature sweeping security lasers that summon elite reinforcements when triggered. In the final Psycho Drive chamber, periodic purple energy pulses radiate across the entire floor, forcing players to leap onto hanging gantries to avoid full-screen damage.
   * Enemies: Heavily armored cyborg shock troops, mech-suit brutes, and veteran Doll bodyguards.
   * Boss Encounter: Final Form M. Bison (Overload). Bison absorbs the Psycho Drive, unleashing screen-filling flaming Psycho Crushers, telekinetic grabs that throw players across the room, and shadow decoys.
 * Stage 12: Akuma's Volcano (Secret 1CC Stage)
   * Setting & Atmosphere: The cracking caldera of Gokuentou Island, unlocked strictly by completing the game without using a continue.
   * Hazards & Flow: Narrow volcanic platforms bordered by instant-kill magma pools. Volcanic rocks rain down in marked drop shadows. There are zero standard mobs—the stage consists entirely of Akuma's master disciples serving as mini-bosses.
   * Boss Encounter: Akuma (Roman Reigns). Unyielding offensive pressure with air Gohadoukens and flaming hurricane kicks across two full health bars. When the screen flashes black to signal the startup of the Shun Goku Satsu (Raging Demon), players must jump immediately to avoid an unblockable instant knockout.
