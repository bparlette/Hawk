# Toad City Baltimore — design (Brad, 2026-10-10)
Base engine: K · Toad Street 32 (/workspace/work/ss3d-bakeoff/k_toad32), a fork of J Cinema City: HD cel-shaded look by default (Brad picked HD on Oct 10 2026; the 32-bit PS1 look stays only as a ?retro=1 toggle), toad couple (player = bald husband with cane, AI wife with gray afro and purse), Cinema City camera, QTE and prompts. Layout: tall full screen minus the arcade stick and button area.
World: open-world Baltimore Inner Harbor, real map compressed 3x. Landmarks sit where his low-res map puts them (IMG_8941), with the real map (IMG_8939) as reference. Each landmark is about 1 block from the next on foot. Land runs about 2 blocks deep around the water: walk the promenade along the water seeing one side of the landmarks, and one block in you see their other side.
Landmarks: Oriole Park, M&T Bank Stadium, Science Center, Fed Hill, Domino Sugar, Under Armour, Fort McHenry, Aquarium, Power Plant, Four Seasons, Fells Point, Pendry, Harborplace, USS Constellation, World Trade Center, Washington Monument. No real logos: blank or original signs.
Day/night: bright to dark, flipping every 5 minutes (5 min day to night, 5 min night to day).
Goal: rid the whole city of crime (crime meter per district).
Carry over from Shell Shock 3D: sewers under manholes, metro, skateboard, rooftops, and all the other features. Boat: drive freely on the water and dock or step off at any street edge.
Landmark set pieces:
- Fed Hill: climb for the city view, fight and throw thugs off the hill.
- Domino Sugar: elevator to the roof, fight beside the giant neon sign (original sign art).
- Under Armour: fight in a clothing store.
- M&T Stadium: swarm fight on the field.
- Oriole Park: warehouse brick hallway with a swarm coming down it.
- Aquarium: swim and fight underwater.
- Science Center: IMAX fight with a turtle movie on screen (original film art, not the real TMNT movie).
- Fells Point: drive a car, hanging out both windows, hitting thugs.
- Pendry: relax in the pool, then throw thugs into the harbor.
## Phases (small passes, one per run, checkpoint and deploy each)
1. City blockout: harbor water, land ring, street grid, landmark massings at positions, promenade, collision, 5-min day/night, toads walking and fighting.

## Controls
On touch, the arcade panel uses a ball-top joystick and round arcade buttons like Brad's other games, about 15-20% bigger than in those games. The game sits in the tall area above the panel. No floating virtual pad.
ACT button: one special, biggest, glowing button that changes with context. Near a manhole it drops you into the sewer; near a ladder or wall it climbs or leaps; near a skateboard, car or boat it gets you on or in (press again to get off); otherwise it does a POWER MOVE with a cooldown that floors nearby thugs. Its label changes to match (POWER / SEWER / CLIMB / RIDE / BOARD / DRIVE).

## Fort McHenry set piece: "The Bombardment" (Brad, 2026-10-11)
Replaces the brawl boss arena at Fort McHenry (Admiral Redclaw is unbuilt with Opus in O6; his rig is reused on the flagship deck). Climb onto the fort's ramparts and take a cannon, and the game switches to a first-person, Duck Hunt-style shooting gallery. A British-style fleet of original sailing warships (sloops, bomb ketches, a rocket ship, then Redclaw's flagship) sails across the harbor firing back. Tap or click to aim and fire. You can shoot the ships and their incoming cannonballs and rockets out of the air. It's a playful reenactment of the 1814 bombardment, built around the anthem:
- The Star-Spangled Banner plays throughout (the melody is public domain): a procedural Web Audio fife-and-drum arrangement, never a recording. Its tempo and intensity rise with each wave.
- "Rockets' red glare": rocket-ship waves fire rockets with red streaking trails; shoot them down.
- "Bombs bursting in air": bomb ketches lob mortar shells that arc high and burst overhead; pop them before they land.
- "By the dawn's early light": the fight starts at night and the sky brightens to dawn wave by wave.
- "Our flag was still there": a big flag over the fort is your health bar; hits tatter it. Lose if it falls.
- Win: once the last ship sinks (Redclaw's flagship, with Redclaw on deck as the final target), the full flag raises on the pole to the anthem's last phrase ("o'er the land of the free and the home of the brave"), with fireworks.
- Waves: 1 sloops (cannonballs), 2 bomb ketches (mortars), 3 the rocket ship (red glare), 4 the flagship + Redclaw. 3–4 minutes in all.

### Side quest: Baltimore Bike Life: Excitebike-style side-scrolling racer
Celebrates Baltimore's dirt bike and 4-wheeler culture: big wheelies, Sunday ride-outs, crews riding together. The rivals are a friendly crew, not a thug gang.
- Rides: dirt bike (faster, longer wheelies, easier to crash) or 4-wheeler (steadier, better on rough ground, worse in the air). Mavis can ride too, on a 4-wheeler with her purse strapped on the front.
- Controls: side view with 4 lanes and lane up/down switching. Throttle plus turbo; turbo builds engine heat, and overheating stalls you on the shoulder until it cools. Lean back/forward in the air to set the landing angle: land flat for a speed boost, nose or tail first and you wipe out.
- Wheelie meter: holding a wheelie fills a style meter. Longer wheelies earn more points and a speed boost. Bonus points for wheelies past crowd zones (neighbors cheering on stoops and from windows). Wheelie too long and you flip.
- Courses, one per district on the compressed map: Key Highway (long straights, harbor views, a jump over a pier gap); Fells Point (bumpy cobblestones that shake the bike, tight alleys); Rowhouse block (ramps off stoop steps, formstone walls, a crowd lining the street); Federal Hill (big downhill jump with the skyline behind). Night versions with streetlights and neon.
- Rivals, the Wheelie Kings: a friendly crew of original animal riders who challenge Harold to races. Beat each rider, then their leader, for a custom bike paint job and a crew jacket. Riders can bump each other, and getting bumped mid-air means a crash.
- Extra: a track builder mode. Drag ramps, mud pits, stoops and crowd zones onto a blank street to make your own course (saved locally).
Fit (Hark): entry is an ACT "RIDE OUT" at a crew meetup spot in each district. The Wheelie Kings are original characters, with no real riders, crews or bike brands.
