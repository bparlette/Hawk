Voxel Pokémon in Your Room

Three Pokémon rebuilt as 3D voxel models — one cube per pixel of their game sprites, a full metre tall. Tap a card and they appear in your living room.

Models: voxel builds from PokeAPI game sprites (one cube per pixel, two cubes deep), converted to USDZ with Pixar's USD tools. Local prototyping only.
AR viewing uses Apple Quick Look and needs an iPhone or iPad (iOS 12+).
