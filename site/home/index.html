Open-world brawler · Inner Harbor

Toad City Baltimore

Two tenured mortal normal toads. One harbor full of crime.

An HD cel-shaded open-world brawler on Baltimore's Inner Harbor. Clean it up district by district while day turns to night every 5 minutes.

Play now ▶ 32-bit mode

Plays in your browser, phone or desktop.

The heroes

Harold and Mavis

Retired, married, and done with the harbor's nonsense.

You play him

Harold

Bald, round glasses, cardigan, wooden cane.

  • 3-hit cane combo for every thug in reach.
  • Cane Quake, his POWER move.
AI partner

Mavis

His wife. Gray afro, floral dress, pearls, handbag.

  • Fights beside you and swings her handbag.
  • Hold ACT to revive her when she goes down.

Each boss is its own mini-game
