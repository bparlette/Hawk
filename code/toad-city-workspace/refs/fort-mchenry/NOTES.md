# Fort McHenry - modelling notes

Internal modelling reference only; never ship these photos in the game. Heights/sizes are approximate.

- Footprint: five-pointed star fort (pentagon with bastion points), low grassy earthwork ramparts topped with brick walls, inside a ring ditch; open lawns all around on the tip of the Locust Point peninsula.
- Height: walls ~1-2 stories; inside a parade ground with a ring of 1-2 storey red-brick barracks with white-railed porches and pale roofs.
- Materials/colours: green grass slopes, red brick, white trim, gravel paths, a very tall flagpole with an oversized US flag in the middle; cannons on the ramparts; a sally-port arched gate (fort_2).
- Water: surrounded by water on three sides (Patapsco River); looks back NW toward the Inner Harbor.
- Night: mostly dark; flag floodlit.

Photos: aerial_web_1/2 = star plan (aerial_web_2 has stock watermark, reference only); fort_1 parade ground; fort_2 sally port.
