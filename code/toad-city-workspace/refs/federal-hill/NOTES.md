# Federal Hill Park - modelling notes

Internal modelling reference only; never ship these photos in the game. Heights/sizes are approximate.

- Footprint: a steep, high grassy bluff (~80 ft above the water) on the south side of the basin, flat-topped park with paths, a few trees and a playground; steep slope faces north to the harbor, with a stair up the slope.
- Height: hill ~8 stories tall relative to promenade; rowhouses ring the top on the south/east.
- Materials/colours: green lawn, brick paths, black Civil War cannon on concrete plinths pointing at downtown, a flagpole and a stone monument/obelisk.
- Signature: cannons + panoramic skyline view (perfect "climb for the view" spot); marina/Rash Field below at the foot (park_3).
- Water: north face overlooks the Inner Harbor; Science Center is just west/below.
- Night: view_1 shows the night skyline from the top.

Photos: park_1 cannon & skyline; park_3 aerial of hill + Rash Field + marina.
