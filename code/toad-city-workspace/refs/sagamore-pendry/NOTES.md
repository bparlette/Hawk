# Sagamore Pendry (Recreation Pier) - modelling notes

Internal modelling reference only; never ship these photos in the game. Heights/sizes are approximate.

- Footprint: long rectangular pier building projecting into the water at the foot of Broadway in Fells Point; the street-side head house is the grand facade, the pier shed runs behind it out over the water; inner courtyard (pendry_2); pool deck at the far water end.
- Height: 3-4 stories at the head house; pier sheds 2 stories.
- Materials/colours: red brick with white/cream stone columns, pilasters and balustrades; carved crest in pediment; huge round arch carriage entrance in the base; banners between columns.
- Signature: Beaux-Arts head house with big archway, pool deck with turquoise rectangular pool, navy loungers and a small bar box, looking directly across water to the Domino sign (pool_web_1/2). No rooftop pool exists - the pool is on the pier end at water level.
- Water: pier surrounded by water on 3 sides; street facade faces north onto Thames St.

Photos: Rooftop-specific search failed twice; the pool is on the pier deck instead.
