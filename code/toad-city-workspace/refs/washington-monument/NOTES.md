# Washington Monument (Mount Vernon Place) - modelling notes

Internal modelling reference only; never ship these photos in the game. Heights/sizes are approximate.

- Footprint: square stone base (with fence) at the centre of a cross-shaped arrangement of four park squares (Mount Vernon Place); historic townhouses and a dark Gothic church spire (Methodist church) around it.
- Height: 178 ft column (~16-18 stories) + statue of Washington on top.
- Materials/colours: white/cream marble, plain Doric column, rectangular plinth with inscriptions; black iron fence; bronze equestrian statue nearby; fountain in the south park (monument_1).
- Water: inland, several blocks north of the harbor (up Charles St).
- Night: column floodlit white (mtvernon_2).

Photos: mvp_2 is a vintage postcard showing the square layout.
