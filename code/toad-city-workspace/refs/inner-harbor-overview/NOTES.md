# Inner Harbor overview - modelling notes

Internal modelling reference only; never ship these photos in the game. Heights/sizes are approximate.

- Basin roughly rectangular, open to the east. West side: Harborplace Light St pavilion + Science Center (SW). North: Pratt St pavilion, WTC, Constellation, aquarium piers, Power Plant. South: Federal Hill + marina/Rash Field. East: Harbor East towers, then Fells Point.
- Brick promenade runs right round the water edge.
- aerial_1 = best whole-basin shot (from SE); aerial_2 = marina from above; over_1 = WTC/Constellation corner from the water; over_2 = aquarium footbridge; panorama_1 = night skyline from Federal Hill.
