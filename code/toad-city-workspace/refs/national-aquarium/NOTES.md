# National Aquarium - modelling notes

Internal modelling reference only; never ship these photos in the game. Heights/sizes are approximate.

- Footprint: two buildings on Pier 3 and Pier 4 at the north side of the harbor, linked by a covered footbridge over the water (the red-trussed bridge). Main building: chunky concrete block with steep glass triangular rooftop pyramids.
- Height: ~6-7 stories + glass pyramid peak.
- Materials/colours: raw grey concrete, navy blue and red banded panels, steep sawtooth glass pyramid roofs (green-glowing at night), a colourful mosaic patch of squares on the facade (exterior_3); red-frame glass pavilion at the water.
- Signature: triangular glass pyramid roof; blue wave graphics; drum-shaped blue-and-red marine mammal pavilion (exterior_1).
- Water: on piers, water on three sides; faces south to Science Center and Federal Hill.
- Night: pyramids lit green/purple from inside, blue wave light line (night_1 aerial).

Photos: night_1 is an excellent night aerial of the whole Pier 3/4 area.
