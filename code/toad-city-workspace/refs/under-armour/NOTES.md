# Under Armour (Tide Point HQ / Locust Point) - modelling notes

Internal modelling reference only; never ship these photos in the game. Heights/sizes are approximate.

- Footprint: Tide Point is a campus of 3-4 long old industrial brick buildings on a squared-off pier at the water's edge in Locust Point, just east of Domino; landscaped promenade around the pier edge (tidepoint_web_1 aerial).
- Height: 3-5 stories each; flat roofs, rooftop sign on top.
- Materials/colours: red-brown brick, big multi-pane industrial windows, white trim, rooftop sign in big block capitals on a white frame (use an original brand in game); red accent panels (harboreast_1/2 show a modern grey + red-band building).
- Signature: rooftop block-letter sign, waterfront promenade, bold red-and-black accent colour.
- Water: the pier faces north into the harbor's NW Branch channel, looking at Fells Point / Canton.
- NOTE: no usable photos of the Harbor East brand store were found (search failed twice); the HQ has also moved to Port Covington. Model the generic Tide Point campus.

Photos: tidepoint_web_1 aerial; tidepoint_web_2 rooftop sign; harboreast_1/2 grey+red modern block near Domino.
