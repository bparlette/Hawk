# Four Seasons Baltimore (Harbor East) - modelling notes

Internal modelling reference only; never ship these photos in the game. Heights/sizes are approximate.

- Footprint: tall rectangular glass tower on a podium at the waterfront of Harbor East, between the Legg Mason/Transamerica tower and the condo towers.
- Height: ~22-24 stories (hotel + residences), slim stepped tower.
- Materials/colours: blue-grey/green glass curtain wall, pale tan/limestone podium (fs_web_3 shows stone base with hotel name carved), with a rooftop terrace setback.
- Signature: stepped glass massing; tan stone podium; Harbor East circle with a gold statue (exterior_1).
- Water: south face looks onto the marina/harbor between Inner Harbor and Fells Point.

Photos: fs_web_2 waterfront skyline (Four Seasons is the glass tower); exterior_3 tower close-up; fs_web_3 entrance podium; exterior_2 neighbour towers at night. THIN on clean facade-only views.
