# Dragon paddle boats - modelling notes

Internal modelling reference only; never ship these photos in the game. Heights/sizes are approximate.

- Small 2-4 seat pedal boats with a tall swan-like dragon head and neck at the front; hull ~2.5 m long.
- Colours: glossy green, blue, teal and purple hulls/necks with red/pink spiky fins along the neck and back, yellow/gold eyes and trim.
- Moored in tight rows at a dock beside the Power Plant / aquarium on the north side of the basin.
