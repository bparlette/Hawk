1 · Context

Toad City Baltimore is a phone-first, cel-shaded open-world brawler on Baltimore's Inner Harbor (three.js r160, plain ES modules, everything procedural: no image, model or font files). You play Harold, an elderly toad with a cane; his wife Mavis (grey afro, handbag) fights beside him. Each district has a thug gang; each landmark has a boss. Characters are plain THREE.Group rigs built from primitives with K.part(), posed by code, cel-shaded with ink hulls, then merged by the game's mergeRig() into 2–4 draw calls.

You get one ZIP (Toad_City_Round2_OPUS.zip). It holds the real starter kit (last round it never reached you and you rebuilt one from memory), the real mergeRig, the real poses, Hark's measuring harness, a real-pose viewer, the fixed Harbor Rats reference, the reviews and before/after renders. Bulky material lives in the ZIP; this prompt points at it by file name.

Where we are: gang 1 (Harbor Rats) is final in Hark's fixed version. Gang 2 (Pier Gulls) is getting a v1.1 fix pass in session O1. This session builds gang 4 of 5: the Masked Bandits. Gangs run as separate sessions (O2 Catfish, O3 Bandits, O4 Otters) and may run in parallel.

B. Pier Gulls (gang 2, f1cfba4): good, but three things were not real

Pier Gulls v1 passed its own QA suite (tools/qa.html). Three gaps: - mergeRig was emulated. Your README says "The real mergeRig() was not available to me." It is now in kit/real_mergeRig.js; use only that. - Timings were assumed. You used rate 14 for idle 0.5 s, hurt 0.3 s, windup 0.4 s, attack 0.5 s. The game's real thug timings (src/06_game.js) are:

State Pose call Damping rate Duration / exit
idle / chase idle(t) / run(t, sp) 14 until the AI changes state
windup windup(st) 12 0.5 s (bruiser 0.7 s), then attack
attack attack(st / 0.3) 26 lunge 4 m/s for the first 0.12 s; back to chase at 0.55 s
hurt hurt(st / 0.32) 24 back to chase at 0.34 s
dizzy dizzy(st) 10 until the QTE ends
knock-down hop vy 4.2, gravity 22, then down(st, grounded) 13 getup after 1.25 s (or dying holds down(1, true))
getup getup(st / 0.5) 14 0.5 s, then windup or chase
  • Margins were thin: armed 36 parts / 11,656 tris, grunt_b 11,056, bruiser 11,072 tris (cap 12,000). The far LOD dipped to −2.1 cm in the knock-down.

No real gang colours or symbols, no brand logos, no real people. - Members: each has one silhouette hook (a hat, a prop, a posture). armed carries an original improvised weapon from the stadium lots (a foam finger with a brick inside, a folded camp chair, a pretzel-stand pole). In the real down the rig lies on its back; a bushy tail behind the hips goes straight through the floor unless you follow rule A1. - Rule A1 (only if the tail cannot pass at rest): animateGang must lay the tail flat toward the feet in 'down' and blend it with J.tilt.rotation.x in every other state, exactly like Harbor Rats v2; the README must then say in bold that animateGang is required every frame after applyPose whenever down is used, and give the measured clearance with and without it. - Thin parts: the whiskers and any strap or rod get outline: false, shadow: false. No outlined decorative part may sit mostly inside another outlined part (that is what drew the Harbor Rats belly line). - Style: Harold-level construction (chunky K.G primitives, vertex colours, the toon ramp, ink hulls), big readable heads, mid-value colours, K.INK for black, at most #f2f2f2 for white, readable under the night preset.
