### 4. How the charges violate 46 U.S.C. 41102 and/or 41104(a)

1. **Noncompliant invoice (46 CFR Part 541).** The invoice fails the Commission's billing requirements as follows:
   - Invoice omits required element: Container availability date (imports). (46 CFR 541.6(b)(6); 46 CFR 541.5)
   - Charge last incurred 2026-07-06; invoice issued 2026-08-14 (39 days). Limit is 30 calendar days. (46 CFR 541.7(a))
   Under 46 CFR 541.5 and 541.7, the billed party has no obligation to pay these charges. Demanding and collecting them is alleged to be an unjust and unreasonable practice under 46 U.S.C. 41102(c) and contrary to 46 U.S.C. 41104(a).
2. **Unreasonable demurrage/detention (46 CFR 545.5).** $450.00 was charged for days on which the cargo or equipment could not be moved:
   - MSKU7731044 2026-06-28: terminal closed — $150.00
   - MSKU7731044 2026-06-29: no pickup appointments — $150.00
   - MSKU7731044 2026-06-30: no pickup appointments — $150.00
   These charges cannot serve the incentive principle and are alleged to be unjust and unreasonable under 46 U.S.C. 41102(c). Under 46 U.S.C. 41310(b)(2) the carrier bears the burden of establishing their reasonableness.
3. **Charges outside the applicable free-time terms.** $450.00 was charged for days within free time, outside the clock, duplicated, or above the contract rate:
   - MSKU7731044 2026-06-27: within free time — $150.00
   - MSKU7731044 2026-07-01: within free time — $150.00
   - MSKU7731044 2026-07-02: within free time — $150.00
